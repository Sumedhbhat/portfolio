# Domain glossary

## Portfolio record

The canonical set of authored career facts used to present Sumedh’s professional history. A portfolio record includes identity, employment, work, projects, skills, recognition, and education.

## Domain collection

A cohesive category within the portfolio record. Each collection owns one kind of career fact and may refer to another collection by a stable identifier.

## Projection

A consumer-specific representation derived from the portfolio record. The website, résumé, query tables, and career graph are projections; they do not own career facts.
