# Portfolio prototype verdict

Question: Which content-first layout best presents Sumedh’s complete professional and public body of work?

The Reader’s Edition is now the primary portfolio. Its opening spread leads into a bound-in insert that presents the Query Console and Career Graph as alternate ways to explore the same work. Open `index.html` for the book, or use `?variant=F` or `?variant=G` to link directly to an alternate experience. Each alternate includes a route back to the book.

## Research synthesis

- Brittany Chiang: persistent identity plus a complete scrollable record of experience, projects, and writing.
- Tania Rascia: a personal site as a durable digital garden with explicit content collections.
- Anthony Fu: a large project archive grouped by meaningful categories.
- Lee Robinson: compact introduction; the body of work carries the page.
- Sumedh’s existing portfolio and public GitHub supplied earlier roles and projects absent from the current resume.

## Directions

- E — Reader’s Edition: the primary experience, with a bound-volume concept, opening cover spread, table of contents, chapters, margin notes, a skills bookshelf, and a project catalogue.
- B — Night Shift: a dark, cinematic project timeline with dense technical records and electric accents.
- C — Field Notes: a colorful editorial folio that pairs case-study detail with a magazine-like project archive.
- D — Studio Board: a vivid modular mosaic with bento-like summaries, work cards, and a compact career wall.
- F — Query Console: an alternate working in-browser DuckDB terminal with normalized company, position, and work-point tables plus a résumé-backed `where_i_worked` view.
- G — Career Graph: an alternate Obsidian-inspired force graph connecting employers, role progression, résumé work points, public projects, technologies, recognition, and education.

## Content review before publishing

The earlier Silfra Technologies role came from the previous public portfolio rather than the current resume. Confirm or revise it when a direction is selected.

## Verdict

E is the primary experience. F and G remain intentionally available as alternate readings of the same portfolio rather than competing design directions.

## Professional work layout decision

Casefile Spreads was selected for Chapter I. Its alternating narrative-and-evidence structure gives each system room while keeping measurable outcomes visually prominent. The Annotated Ledger, Impact Index, and comparison switcher were removed after selection.
